yanas_office
Professor Yana's office is small but carefully organized, with a wide desk positioned beneath a bulletin board full 
of schedules, diagrams, and reminders. Several old MacBooks are stacked along one wall, waiting for someone to give 
them another purpose. A set of desk drawers looks ordinary enough, but in a building full of puzzles, even ordinary 
things deserve a closer look. An address book sits among the supplies. The office is quiet except for the soft hum 
of a computer.
You can go east to Fischbach's office or south to Dauber's office.

wests_office
Professor West's office feels noticeably darker than the others. Half-closed blinds let narrow stripes of daylight 
fall across shelves of books and a desk covered in carefully sorted folders. A computer monitor glows in the corner, 
and a clock ticks loudly enough to make the silence uncomfortable. A few drawers are shut tight, while a rolling chair 
faces the empty desk as though someone has just stepped away. The room feels like it might contain a secret, if you knew 
where to look.

Daubers_office
Professor Dauber's office is crowded with computer science textbooks, loose papers, and notes covered in code. A computer
 sits between piles of assignments, and the desk has several drawers that invite a closer inspection. A notebook and a 
 sheet of paper rest among the materials, possibly useful for keeping track of what you discover. A half-erased problem 
 on a nearby whiteboard suggests that someone was working here recently.
You can go north to Yana's office or south to John's office.

johns_office
Professor John's office looks part classroom and part electronics workshop. Old circuit boards, spare cables, adapters, 
and mysterious computer parts occupy nearly every shelf. A tool kit lies open beside a machine that appears to have been 
taken apart and not quite put back together. The desk is cluttered, but everything seems to have a purpose. If the building 
has a technological puzzle, this would be a sensible place to search for ideas.
You can go north to Dauber's office or south to Classroom 2.

restroom
This second-floor restroom is strangely still. Several stall doors stand partly open, while the mirrors above the sinks 
reflect the harsh light of a flickering ceiling fixture. A trash can sits beside the entrance, and one faucet drips at 
regular intervals. The tiled floor makes every step echo, making the room seem larger and emptier than it really is. 
Nothing immediately looks out of place, but you might want to check carefully.
You can go west to Classroom 2 or east to the other second-floor restroom.

classroom1
Classroom 1 is arranged in neat rows of desks facing a whiteboard covered with faded marker lines. A projector hangs from 
the ceiling, and a teacher's desk at the front has several drawers. Papers left near the board hint at a lesson that ended 
in a hurry. Sunlight filters through the windows, lighting up the dust over the empty seats. It seems like the kind of place 
where an overlooked assignment could be hiding.
You can go north to Fischbach's office, south to the second-floor stairs, or west to Classroom 5.

classroom2
Classroom 2 is a computer lab filled with workstations, keyboards, and rolling chairs. Most of the screens are dark, but the indicator 
lights on a few machines still glow. The instructor's desk holds a keyboard, scattered notes, and drawers full of supplies. The faint sound
 of cooling fans fills the room, making it easy to imagine a class just finishing a programming exercise. A useful clue could be sitting in plain 
 sight.

classroom3
Classroom 3 contains long rows of desks facing a wide whiteboard and a ceiling-mounted projector. Several chairs are pushed out of place, as if students
 left in a hurry. At the back of the room stands a locked cabinet, its metal handle worn from years of use. A pile of old worksheets rests on the teacher's 
 desk. The locked cabinet is worth remembering, even if you cannot open it right now.

Fischbachs_office
Professor Fischbach's office is almost suspiciously tidy. Books line the shelves in perfect order, and a few New Jersey souvenirs are displayed beside framed
 photographs. The desk is polished, with hardly a paper out of place. A small thumb drive lies among the items on the desk, easy to miss unless you pay attention.
  Everything looks normal at first glance, but the careful arrangement makes you wonder what deserves a second look.
You can go west to Yana's office or south to Classroom 1.

WU_Cast_station
The WU Cast Station is packed with microphones, headphones, recording equipment, and computers ready for the next broadcast. Notes and tangled cables cover a small
 desk, while soundproofing panels soften the noise of the building outside. A recording light above one workstation is dark, but the room still feels ready to go live
  at any moment. You might learn something by examining what the previous crew left behind.
You can go north to the TV Studio or south to the lobby.

TV_studio
The TV Studio opens into a large production space filled with cameras, tripods, bright lights, and movable backdrops. Tape marks on the floor 
show where presenters are supposed to stand, and a teleprompter faces the main camera. Heavy cables run toward the walls, connecting the equipment
 to the control room. With no crew around, the studio feels like a set waiting for its next scene. Even ordinary equipment looks mysterious under the 
 silent lights.
You can go west to the Control Room or south to the WU Cast Station.
#
Control_room
The Control Room overlooks the TV Studio through a wide glass window. Several monitors show empty camera views, while a console crowded with switches,
 faders, and buttons stretches across the desk. Headsets hang beside the workstations, and bundles of audio and video cables disappear behind the equipment racks.
  This is clearly where the broadcasts are managed. A careful explorer might find that the equipment offers more information than the room first suggests.
You can go east to the TV Studio or south to the lecture hall.

Lecture_hall
The Lecture Hall is spacious, with rows of seats descending toward a podium and a large projection screen. A microphone rests at the front, and faded notes 
are still visible on a side whiteboard. The overhead lights give the empty room a quiet, almost theatrical feeling. Every footstep carries across the seats.
A forgotten handout near the podium suggests someone left before finishing their work.
You can go north to the Control Room or south to Classroom 3.
#
Classroom_5
Classroom 5 is a quiet teaching space with desks arranged in small groups. A whiteboard fills the front wall, and a computer station sits beside a teacher's
 desk fitted with several drawers. A few marker caps and scraps of paper have been left behind. The room looks ordinary, yet its silence makes you notice every
  detail. If you are searching for something connected to a class assignment, checking the desk would make sense.
You can go east to Classroom 1.
#
Restroom_2
The first-floor restroom looks as if the cleaning crew has not visited in a while. Water spots cover the large mirror, a paper towel lies near the sinks, and
 one stall door creaks whenever it moves. The lights buzz overhead, and the air has the faint smell of old cleaning products. It is not the most inviting place
  in Freedom Hall, but even an unpleasant room might hide something useful.
You can go west to Classroom 3 or east to the lobby.
#
lobby
The first-floor lobby is the main gathering space of Freedom Hall. Notice boards display campus announcements, student events, and flyers that have started
 to peel at the corners. Comfortable chairs sit near the walls, and footsteps echo across the open floor. From here, different parts of the building branch 
 off in several directions. It feels like the natural place to get your bearings before continuing the search for a way out.
You can go west to the first-floor restroom, north to the WU Cast Station, or east to the first-floor stairs.
#
upstairs_restroom_2
This is the second restroom on the upper floor. Its clean tile, bright mirror, and evenly spaced sinks make it feel newer than the restroom next door.
 Several stall doors are closed, and a hand dryer occasionally rattles when the air shifts. The quiet atmosphere might be reassuring if you were not trying
  to escape a locked building. For now, nothing here seems to explain how to get outside.
You can go west to the other second-floor restroom or east to the second-floor stairs.
#
First Floor Stairs
A concrete staircase rises from the first floor toward the offices and classrooms above. Metal handrails run along the walls, and an emergency exit sign 
casts a faint glow over the landing. The sound of your footsteps carries upward through the stairwell. From here, you can return to the familiar first-floor
 lobby or head upstairs to explore more of Freedom Hall.
You can go west to the lobby or up to the second-floor stairs.
#
Second Floor Stairs
The second-floor stairwell connects the upper offices and classrooms with the floor below. A sturdy railing circles the landing, and the light from a narrow
 window stretches across the steps. You can hear the building's ventilation system humming behind the walls.
You can go north to Classroom 1, west to the second upstairs restroom, or down to the first-floor stairs.
